Government Exam Photo & Signature Guidelines

This tool is specially designed for Indian competitive exam aspirants to compress and resize photos and signatures down to exact KB limits (such as 20KB or 50KB) without losing clarity or sharpness.

Supported Examinations

  • SSC Exams: CGL, CHSL, GD Constable, MTS, CPO, Steno (Photo: 20KB-50KB, Signature: 10KB-20KB)
  • Banking Exams: IBPS PO, Clerk, SBI PO, RBI Assistant
  • Railway Exams: RRB NTPC, Group D, ALP, Technician
  • UPSC & State PSC: IAS, IPS, State PCS, Police Recruitment
