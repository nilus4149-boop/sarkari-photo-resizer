Why File Size and Dimension Restrictions Matter in Government Forms

When millions of candidates submit applications simultaneously on servers hosted by bodies like the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) or Union Public Service Commission (UPSC), server bandwidth and database storage become critical constraints. To prevent server crashes and ensure fast loading times, recruitment portals enforce strict rules:

  • Strict KB Caps: Most portals demand passport photos under 50KB or 20KB, and signatures under 20KB or 30KB.
  • Clear Readability: Even under compressed file sizes, automated verification scripts and human examiners must clearly recognize the applicant's face and signature strokes.
  • Format Compatibility: Portals generally accept standard JPEG or JPG formats. PNG files with transparent backgrounds often throw errors during upload.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What should I do if my photo size is still too large after compression?

If the target KB size is extremely low (such as 10KB or 15KB) and your original image resolution is massive (e.g., 4000x3000 pixels), try cropping out extra background space using any basic image viewer before uploading it to our tool. Cropping out unwanted background reduces pixel weight significantly.

2. Is this tool safe for uploading confidential documents and signatures?

Yes, absolutely. Because the resizing process happens entirely inside your browser memory via HTML5 and JavaScript, your photos never leave your device.

3. What image formats are supported by government recruitment portals?

Almost all Indian government job portals (including SSC, UPSC, IBPS, and state boards) strictly require .jpg or .jpeg formats. Our tool automatically exports your final output in standard JPEG format.

4. Why do government portals reject photos even when the size is under the limit?

Rejection usually occurs due to incorrect dimensions (aspect ratio), blurry focus, wearing sunglasses/caps, or having a dark/patterned background instead of a plain white or light-colored background. Always ensure your passport photo has a clean background and clear lighting.
