Why File Size and Dimension Restrictions Matter in Government Portals

When millions of candidates submit application forms simultaneously on web servers hosted by government examining bodies like the SSC or UPSC, server bandwidth, database traffic, and storage capacity become critical technical constraints. To prevent server crashes during peak registration hours and ensure lightning-fast page loading times for reviewers, recruitment portals enforce strict file compliance rules:

  • Strict KB Caps: Most government recruitment portals demand passport-sized photographs under 50KB or 20KB, and candidate signatures strictly under 10KB or 20KB to save database bandwidth.
  • Clear Readability and Verification: Even under compressed file sizes, automated verification scripts and human board examiners must clearly recognize the applicant's facial features, ears, eyes, and signature strokes without blurriness.
  • Format Compatibility: Portals generally accept standard JPEG, JPG, or PDF formats. PNG files containing transparent backgrounds or unoptimized raw smartphone formats frequently throw errors or get rejected during upload.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What should I do if my photo size is still slightly large after compression?

If the target KB limit requested by the exam portal is extremely low (such as 10KB or 15KB) and your original image resolution is massive (e.g., a 4000x3000 pixel camera shot), try cropping out extra background space using any basic image viewer before uploading it to our tool. Cropping out unwanted background surroundings reduces pixel data weight significantly while preserving facial clarity.

2. Is this tool safe for uploading confidential documents and signatures?

Yes, absolutely 100% safe. Because the resizing and conversion process happens entirely inside your browser memory via HTML5 canvas and JavaScript, your personal photos and signatures never leave your local device.

3. What image formats are supported by Indian government recruitment portals?

Almost all Indian government job portals (including Staff Selection Commission, Union Public Service Commission, Institute of Banking Personnel Selection, and various state recruitment boards) strictly require .jpg, .jpeg, or .pdf formats. Our tool automatically exports your final output in either standard JPEG or PDF format according to your dropdown selection.

4. Why do government portals reject photos even when the file size is under the limit?

Rejection usually occurs due to incorrect aspect ratio dimensions, blurry focus, wearing sunglasses or caps, or having a dark, patterned, or textured background instead of a plain white or light-colored background. Always ensure your passport photograph has clean background lighting and clear visibility.
