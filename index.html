Govt Form Photo & KB Resizer

Easily compress your photo and signature to exact KB sizes for government exams.

About Govt Form Photo & KB Resizer Tool

When applying for online government jobs, sarkari exams, or admissions, portals often require passport-sized photos and signatures to be under a specific file size limit (such as 20KB, 50KB, or 100KB). This free online tool helps you instantly compress your images without losing essential clarity.

How to Use This Tool:

  1. Click on 'Choose File' and select your photo or signature from your device.
  2. Enter your desired maximum file size in KB (Kilobytes).
  3. Click on the Compress & Download Image button to save your resized file.
